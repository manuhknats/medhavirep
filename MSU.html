06

On-the-Job Learning — OJL

OJL connects your academic learning with practical workplace experience under industry guidance.

01

Collect Your LMS Login & Logbook

Collect the required learning and OJL records from the faculty in charge.

02

Join Your Department

Report to your allocated department and instructor.

03

Record Daily Learning

Maintain your logbook with tasks, tools, flowcharts and key learnings.

04

Get Monthly Feedback

Your supervisor should provide feedback at the end of every month.

05

Submit for Review

Submit your logbook to the faculty for regular evaluation.

07

How to Maintain Your OJL Logbook

OJL LOGBOOK
Date Daily entry
Task Work performed
Tools Tools / equipment used
Process Process / flowchart
Learning Key learning / observation

Remember

  • Make entries regularly.
  • Record actual work performed.
  • Clearly mention tools and processes.
  • Capture important learnings.
  • Get regular review and feedback.
